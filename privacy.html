Privacy Policy

G Mortar Trainer · Last updated October 19, 2026

G Mortar Trainer is made by Christopher T Mitchell, an independent developer. In short: the app does not collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter or create in the app (your name and settings, Gunner's Exam rosters and scores, and FDC scenarios) is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own.

No analytics, tracking, or ads

The app contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no cookies. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The FDC Trainer subscription is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment card details, your name, or your Apple ID. The app asks Apple only whether this device's Apple ID has an active subscription.

Things you choose to share

Some actions send information only when you choose to, and only to where you choose:

  • Exports: PDFs and backups you export or share go only where you send them, for example by email or AirDrop.
  • Copy Diagnostics: in Help, this copies the app version, device and screen information, storage usage, and the app's recent error log to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it into a message yourself.
  • Emailing support: if you email us, we receive only what you send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't share it.
  • Crash reports: if you have turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. This is controlled by you, through Apple.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored with Personal Settings → Clear All Data, or by deleting the app. Your subscription is kept by Apple and can be brought back with Restore Purchases.

Children

The app is a military training aid for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
