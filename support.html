Reporting a problem

In the app, open Help & FAQ and tap Copy Diagnostics. Paste it into an email to the address above, along with what you were doing when the problem happened. The diagnostics include the app version, device information, and the app's recent error log — nothing personal from your training data.

Restoring your subscription

On a new or reset device, open Personal Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases (it's also on the FDC Trainer subscription screen). Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancelling your subscription

Open your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose the FDC Trainer subscription, and tap Cancel. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Personal Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (including a free trial) to avoid being charged for the next one.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
