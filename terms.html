Disclaimer

This application is not endorsed, built, funded, or affiliated with the Department of War (DoW), U.S. Army, or U.S. Government. It is an independent personal project. This is strictly a training aid and is not intended to replace any current FDC computer systems. Ballistic outputs are training approximations only — NOT FOR LIVE FIRE. Firing data is computed by a simplified ballistic model calibrated against public-release Army doctrine (TC 3-22.90, TC 3-22.91) to within ±10 mils at published 81mm and 120mm reference points; 60mm is validated on maximum range only. It is not derived from official firing tables. Times of flight and time settings are approximate.
